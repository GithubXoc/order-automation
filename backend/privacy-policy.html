Privacy Policy

Test Furniture Shop – effective November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Test Furniture Shop (“we”, “us”) collects when you message our Facebook Page, Order automation test page, how we use it, and the choices you have. Our order assistant connects to Facebook Messenger so we can answer your questions and handle furniture orders more quickly.

Information we collect

When you send a message to our Page, Meta passes the following to our system:

  • Your Page-scoped ID, a number Facebook assigns to you for our Page only. It is not your Facebook account ID and cannot be used to find your profile elsewhere.
  • The content of your messages, including text, photos and files you send us.
  • The time each message was sent.
  • Your public profile name, if Meta makes it available to us.

If you place an order, we also keep the details you give us in the conversation, such as the furniture you want, sizes, materials, delivery address, phone number and delivery date.

We do not collect your Facebook password, friends list, posts or any information you have not sent to our Page.

How we use your information

  • To read and reply to your messages.
  • To prepare quotes, take orders, make your furniture and arrange delivery.
  • To keep a record of what was agreed, so we can answer questions about your order later.
  • To improve how our assistant understands and answers customer messages.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Automated replies and AI

We use an AI service, Muse by Meta, to help read incoming messages, pull out order details and draft replies. Your message content is sent to this service only for that purpose. Our staff review and can correct replies, and you can always ask to speak with a person.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with:

  • Meta, which operates Facebook Messenger and delivers messages between you and us.
  • The AI service named above, to process messages.
  • Hosting and storage providers that run our system on our behalf.
  • Delivery partners, when needed to deliver your order (name, address and phone number only).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversations and order records for up to 12 months after your last message or completed order, unless we need them longer for an open order, a warranty claim or legal record-keeping. After that, we delete them.

How we protect it

Your information is stored on secured servers, and only the people who handle your messages and orders can access it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you at any time.

  1. Send a message to Order automation test page on Messenger saying “Delete my data”, or email bhosoo0523@gmail.com.
  2. We will delete your messages and personal details from our system within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

If you have an order in progress, we may need to keep the details required to finish it, then delete them afterwards. Deleting data from our system does not remove the conversation from your own Messenger app or from Meta’s records; you can manage those in your Facebook settings.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

Our service is meant for adults placing furniture orders. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced on our Facebook Page.
